Hi, I'm Dami 👋🏽

Hi, I'm Amodu Damilola, and i am a front-end developer based in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. I’m currently studying Software Engineering at NIIT to improve my skills. Before this, I worked at Ruldef Computers (2020–2022) as a Computer Operator and Graphics Designer.

I create visual designs that speak for brands—logos, flyers, social media graphics, and more. I mostly work with CorelDRAW, Photoshop, and Illustrator, and I enjoy turning simple ideas into designs that grab attention and feel professional. Whether it’s for print or digital, I always aim for clean, well-balanced visuals that reflect the message behind them. Although i am not currently looking for a full-time role in graphics design, but I’m also open to freelance work if the project feels like a good fit

Check out my work in the Projects section – I’ve shared a few designs I created while learning and growing. Let’s connect if you’d like to collaborate!
